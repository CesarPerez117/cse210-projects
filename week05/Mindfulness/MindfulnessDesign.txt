Responsibilities

* Activity
    - Will get from user the time for the activity
    - Show the spinner in the reflection activity
    - The get ready will show a timer and start the activity

* BreathingActivity
    - The Breathing method will provide a decent time to
    breath between and will prompt instructions to the user.

* ReflectionActivity
    - The methods will get a promtp from the list, then provide a 
    question and time for the user to think about.

* ListingActivity
    - The method GetUserList will get all the things the user
    will write down as the timer goes. The prompt provided will
    be randomly taken from the list of prompts.
